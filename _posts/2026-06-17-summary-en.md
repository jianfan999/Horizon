## [Custom PRNG in Slay the Spire 2 for Seed Consistency](https://tck.mn/blog/correlated-randomness-sts2/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Andy Tockman's article explains why Slay the Spire 2 implements a custom pseudo-random number generator (PRNG) to avoid correlated randomness and guarantee cross-platform seed consistency, a problem that plagued the original game. This ensures that the same seed produces identical gameplay across all platforms, preserving fairness and enabling players to share runs. It also highlights the importance of deterministic RNG in game design, especially for multiplayer or speedrunning contexts. The custom PRNG prevents correlation between multiple random number generators initialized from the same seed by using independent states, avoiding patterns that made some seeds unwinnable in Slay the Spire. The implementation is platform-independent and algorithmically deterministic.

hackernews · rdmuser · Jun 16, 09:46 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48552844)

**Background**: In video games, pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs) produce sequences of numbers that appear random but are determined by a starting seed. Different platforms may use different PRNG implementations, causing the same seed to yield different outcomes. Correlated randomness occurs when multiple PRNGs are initialized with the same state, leading to undesirable patterns. Implementing a custom PRNG solves these issues by providing consistent, reproducible randomness across all environments.
