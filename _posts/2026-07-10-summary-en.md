## [No Leap Second at End of 2026, IERS Announces](https://datacenter.iers.org/data/latestVersion/bulletinC.txt) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service (IERS) has announced that no leap second will be added to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) at the end of December 2026. This announcement affects global timekeeping systems, as leap seconds are used to synchronize atomic time with Earth's rotational time, and their removal or continuation is a key technical and policy decision for navigation, communication, and computing infrastructure. The IERS Bulletin C specifies that the difference between UTC and UT1 (astronomical time) will remain within ±0.9 seconds, requiring no adjustment. The current UTC-TAI offset remains at -37 seconds, and the UTC-GPS offset stays at -18 seconds.

hackernews · ChrisArchitect · Jul 9, 14:16 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48846281)

**Background**: A leap second is an occasional adjustment added to UTC to keep it within 0.9 seconds of astronomical time (UT1), which varies due to irregularities in Earth's rotation. The IERS monitors Earth's rotation and decides when to insert or remove leap seconds. Since 1972, 27 leap seconds have been added, but the slowing of Earth's rotation has become less predictable, leading to discussions about abolishing leap seconds in favor of a continuous time scale.

## [Running GLM 5.2 on a 32GB RAM laptop with Colibri](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A developer successfully runs the 744B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model GLM 5.2 on a laptop with only 32GB RAM using int4 quantization, Multi-Token Prediction (MTP), and Dynamic Sparse Attention (DSA) via a custom CPU-only engine called Colibri, achieving about 0.1 tokens per second. This breakthrough shows that even consumer-grade hardware can run large Mixture-of-Experts LLMs locally, making privacy-preserving and offline AI inference accessible to a wider audience without requiring expensive GPUs. Colibri keeps the dense part of GLM 5.2 (~17B parameters) resident in RAM at int4 precision (~9.9 GB) while streaming the 21,504 routed experts from disk (~370 GB) on demand with an LRU cache. The engine is a single C file (about 1,300 lines) with no GPU or BLAS dependencies, entirely CPU-based.

hackernews · vforno · Jul 9, 08:05 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48842459)

**Background**: GLM 5.2 is a large Mixture-of-Experts model with 744B total parameters but activates only ~40B per token. int4 quantization reduces the memory footprint of models by storing weights as 4-bit integers. MTP allows predicting multiple future tokens at once to speed up inference, while DSA reduces the context window size. Colibri is a minimal inference engine designed for CPU-only execution.
