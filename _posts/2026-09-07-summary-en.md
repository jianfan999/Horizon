## [Asahi Linux Announces Official Apple M3 Support](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Asahi Linux announced official support for Apple's M3 chip, extending its Linux-on-Apple-Silicon effort to the latest generation of Macs. The milestone builds on earlier support for M1 and M2 series devices. This marks a major technical achievement for running Linux on Apple Silicon, bringing community-driven open-source support to Apple's newest hardware. It strengthens Asahi Linux's position as the primary path for Linux users who want modern Apple hardware without macOS. The Asahi Linux team previously shipped support for M1 Ultra and Mac Studio in July 2022, with early M2 support arriving at the same time. Booting Microsoft Windows remains out of scope because it would require modifying the proprietary Windows kernel.

hackernews · mdp2021 · Sep 6, 14:08 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)

**Background**: Asahi Linux is a community project dedicated to porting Linux to Apple Silicon Macs, starting with the M1 models released in 2020. Because Apple's chips and firmware are proprietary, Asahi developers must reverse-engineer hardware components and provide their own open-source drivers. The project began releasing usable updates in 2022, and has been gradually expanding support to newer Apple chips.

## [A/I Collective Shuts Down After US Terror Sanctions](https://keepitfree.ai/announcements/a/i-shuts-down-stay-human/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The A/I Collective (Autistici/Inventati), an Italian-provider of secure communication and hosting services, announced its shutdown in September 2026 after the US government added it to the specially designated global terrorist list in August 2026. The collective cited legal and financial risks from the sanctions, and the autistici.org domain became unreachable as a result. This shows how state sanctions can force privacy-focused and independent hosting providers offline, raising serious concerns about free speech and the ability of activists to communicate securely. It also signals a chilling effect for autonomous technical infrastructure that supports dissidents and marginalized groups. The autistici.org domain was placed under serverHold at the .org registry, making it unreachable, and the Noblogs blogging platform was later compromised and scraped into a public searchable database. The US government accused A/I of designing services that facilitated violent attacks, an accusation the collective disputed before shutting down.

hackernews · captainmuon · Sep 6, 14:34 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586898)

**Background**: Autistici/Inventati (A/I) was founded in 2001 by members of the anti-globalisation movement in Italy and provided email, messaging, videoconferencing, web hosting, and the Noblogs publishing platform to left-wing activists who opposed fascism, militarism, racism, sexism, homophobia, and transphobia. The collective had a history of standing up to Italian police wiretapping, court-ordered takedowns, and Norwegian police data seizures before the US sanctions made continued operation legally and financially untenable.

## [NetBSD 9.5 Released, Marking End of Life for NetBSD 9](https://blog.netbsd.org/tnf/entry/netbsd_9_5_released_and) ⭐️ 6.0/10

NetBSD 9.5 has been released as the final maintenance release of the NetBSD 9 series. The NetBSD project has also declared the NetBSD 9 branch end-of-life, so it will no longer receive security or maintenance updates. Users still running NetBSD 9 systems must plan upgrades to a supported release to continue receiving security fixes. This matters for the BSD community because it marks the transition of older deployments to the current NetBSD branch, especially on older hardware that may require careful migration. NetBSD 9.5 is the last release to include bug fixes for the 9.x series, and after this point NetBSD 9 will not receive any further updates. Users on NetBSD 9 are advised to upgrade to a newer supported release rather than staying on 9.5.

hackernews · jaypatelani · Sep 6, 15:44 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49587636)

**Background**: NetBSD is a free, open-source Unix-like operating system noted for its portability across many hardware architectures and platforms, from small embedded systems to older desktop machines. It uses the permissive BSD license and includes the pkgsrc package collection with thousands of third-party software packages. In software lifecycle terms, end-of-life means the project stops publishing security updates and bug fixes for that release branch, so continued use can become a security risk.

The Moody's analysis focuses on hyperscalers, the largest cloud service providers, and compares their AI-related investments and resulting physical compute capacity. The report does not say Chinese firms match US absolute capacity, but rather that their per-dollar efficiency narrows the overall gap.

In the current AI boom, access to massive computing power is widely seen as critical for training large models. The Moody's report suggests that in China, factors like domestic hardware pricing and state-backed support make compute procurement cheaper, meaning similar compute budgets can go further.

## [China's Fengyun-4C lightning satellite aids China-Nepal flood relief](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3366535/worlds-most-powerful-lightning-chasing-satellite-joins-china-nepal-flood-relief-efforts?utm_source=rss_feed) ⭐️ 6.0/10

China is using the Fengyun-4C (FY-4C) satellite, described as the world's most powerful geostationary weather satellite, to track severe thunderstorms over Nepal and provide real-time data for ongoing China-Nepal rescue operations after last month's glacier collapse. The satellite's Lightning Mapping Imager (LMI) operates at very high temporal resolution to pinpoint lightning activity. This demonstrates how China's newest weather satellite capabilities can be applied to international disaster relief, particularly for improving early warnings of flash floods and landslides. It also underscores the value of collaborative satellite monitoring in Himalayan border regions where severe weather frequently triggers disasters. The FY-4C was launched on December 26, 2025, aboard a Long March 3B rocket from the Xichang Satellite Launch Center and operates at 36,000 km (22,000 miles) above the equator. Its Lightning Mapping Imager captures 500 frames per second and sifts through 30 gigabits of data every second to detect both intra-cloud and cloud-to-ground lightning.

rss · SCMP · Sep 6, 08:47

**Background**: Fengyun-4 (FY-4) is China's second-generation geostationary meteorological satellite series, operated by the China Meteorological Administration. Lightning Mapping Imagers on FY-4 satellites continuously observe lightning flashes even under daytime and nighttime conditions, helping forecasters identify rapidly intensifying storms and issue earlier severe weather warnings. The Nepal flooding followed a devastating glacier collapse, so high-resolution storm monitoring has become critical for rescue and disaster response planning.
