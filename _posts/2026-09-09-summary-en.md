## [AlphaGenome Atlas: High-Resolution Predictive Map of Human DNA Variants](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/alphagenome-atlas/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Google DeepMind has released the AlphaGenome Atlas, a catalogue of molecular effect predictions and AVI scores for 9 billion single-nucleotide variants across the human genome. It is based on AlphaGenome, a unified DNA sequence model that takes 1 Mb of DNA as input. The atlas provides scientists with a high-resolution, predictive resource that can help interpret both coding and non-coding variants, many of which are linked to disease. It advances AI-driven genomics and could accelerate research into pathogenic mutations and regulatory mechanisms. The atlas covers single-nucleotide variants across all possible DNA letter changes, with predictions including molecular effects and AVI scores. A web portal lets researchers explore predictions; non-coding DNA, including promoter sequences, is part of the dataset.

hackernews · utiiiD · Sep 8, 14:55 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49611251)

**Background**: AlphaGenome is a DNA sequence model designed to overcome the trade-off between input sequence length and prediction resolution, enabling interpretation of large non-coding regions. The human genome is mostly non-coding, and variants in those regions are linked to many diseases. This model builds on DeepMind's earlier AlphaFold success in protein structure prediction.
